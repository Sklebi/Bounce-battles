# Bounce-battle

This is Split-Bounce!

There are two frogs (red & blue) that are trying to work together to get the highest combined score.
You and your duo have to use the WASD and Arrow Keys to try and work together
If you look on the left and the right, the scores are shown. The left side is the score for the rhythm game and the right side is the score for the platform game.

This is how you play: 

Get Two People! (Or try to play both if you think you can handle it)

One person controls the wsad keys, and the other person controls the arrow keys.

When the swap bounce timer hits zero, swap what game your controlling! 

Bounce between each game and see your highest score!
